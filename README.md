## About

Senior Full-Stack Software Engineer with **10+ years** building high-performance, scalable and secure systems across public administration, e-commerce, real-time communication platforms, omnichannel solutions and large enterprise portals.

My work centers on three things: **designing distributed architectures** that survive real traffic, **modernizing legacy systems** without stopping the business, and **squeezing performance out of databases** that were never meant to scale. I've shipped mission-critical applications handling millions of operations, led engineering teams, and mentored developers along the way.

Currently focused on integrating **AI into production systems** — agents, RAG pipelines and automation that solve concrete business problems instead of demos.

## What I'm Building

### [Alto Vale IA](https://altovaleia.com) — `altovaleia.com`

AI solutions hub for small and mid-sized businesses in the Alto Vale do Itajaí region (SC, Brazil). Co-founder and CTO, responsible for product architecture, cloud infrastructure and client delivery.

- Custom AI agents and RAG systems built on top of client data
- Process automation pipelines (n8n, Make, event-driven workers)
- Applied AI training and consulting for regional companies
- Stack: **NestJS · Next.js · PostgreSQL · Supabase · Redis · BullMQ · Docker**

### [Alto Vale Shopping](https://altovaleshopping.com) — `altovaleshopping.com`

Regional digital commerce platform connecting local merchants to online customers in the Alto Vale do Itajaí.

- Multi-tenant storefront architecture with per-merchant catalogs
- Automated content and social distribution pipeline with human approval gate
- Product ingestion, catalog sync and integration APIs
- Stack: **Next.js · TypeScript · PostgreSQL · Supabase · Tailwind**
